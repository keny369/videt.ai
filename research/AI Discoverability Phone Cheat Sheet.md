# AI Discoverability Phone Cheat Sheet**

## **The Three Ways AI Can Hurt a Business**

Imagine a traffic light.

🔴 **Wrong**
 🟡 **Missing**
 🟢 **Losing**

Every report fits into one of these.

------

## **🔴 Hook 1: You’re Being Described Incorrectly**

### **Psychology**

People hate being misrepresented.

This is about **reputation**.

Examples

• Wrong address

• Invented qualification

• Fake employee

• Wrong office

• Incorrect phone number

The prospect thinks:

“That’s wrong.”

Not

“Interesting AI.”

Just

“Fix that.”

### **Product**

**Product A**

We find factual errors in how AI describes your business.

### **Conversation**

“What happens if a prospective client asks ChatGPT about your business tomorrow?”

------

## **🟡 Hook 2: AI Doesn’t Know You Exist**

### **Psychology**

People fear becoming invisible.

Nobody wants to disappear.

Examples

Someone asks

“Who are the best buyers’ advocates in Melbourne?”

Five competitors appear.

They don’t.

Nothing is wrong.

They’re simply absent.

This is today’s equivalent of not appearing in Google.

Except there is no second page.

### **Product**

**Product B**

We measure whether AI knows you exist when buyers ask questions.

### **Conversation**

“You’re not being represented incorrectly.

You’re not being represented at all.”

------

## **🟢 Hook 3: Your Competitor Owns the Conversation**

### **Psychology**

People hate losing.

Especially market leaders.

Examples

Every recommendation contains

Competitor A

Competitor A

Competitor A

Sometimes Competitor B

Never you.

The problem isn’t accuracy.

The problem is market share.

### **Product**

**Product C**

We measure who AI recommends instead of you.

### **Conversation**

“The AI has already started choosing winners.”

# **When They Ask**

## **“Can this be fixed?”**

Answer

Yes. Almost every issue we’ve found has a path to improvement.

------

## **“So how do we fix it?”**

Answer

The fix depends entirely on why the AI produced that result. Two companies can have exactly the same symptom but require completely different solutions. The report you’ve seen is the diagnosis. The implementation plan is a separate piece of work.

------

## **“Can you just tell me generally?”**

Answer

Broadly speaking, there are four places we usually work:

• your own website

• the wider web where AI learns about you

• the consistency of information across trusted sources

• the evidence that establishes your authority in your market

Which of those matters most depends on what caused your specific result.

------

## **“Why can’t you just tell me the fix now?”**

Answer

It’s a bit like medicine. Two people can both have a headache, but one is dehydrated and the other has the flu. The symptom is the same. The treatment isn’t. We diagnose first, then we build the treatment plan.

------

## **“So you’re saying there isn’t one magic fix?”**

Answer

Exactly. If there were one universal fix, everyone would already be doing it. Our job is identifying which changes will actually move the needle for your business, rather than having you spend months working on things that won’t.

------

# **The Sales Mindset**

This may be the most important page.

You are **not selling SEO**.

You are **not selling AI optimisation**.

You are **not selling prompts**.

You are selling **certainty**.

Anyone can search for “How do I rank in ChatGPT?”

The internet is full of advice.

What nobody knows is:

**Which advice actually matters for my business?**

That’s what they’re buying.
