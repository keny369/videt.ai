# Videt Founding 20: Target List v2

### Ten names. Two verticals. Melbourne. August 2026.

Replaces v1 (family law and dentistry). Chosen on fee-per-client, a named reachable principal, and a structural visibility hook that was directly observed rather than assumed.

------

## Vertical 1: Buyer's advocates

**Fee per client:** roughly $15k to $25k, or 2 to 2.5 percent of purchase price. One referred client pays for a decade of membership.

**The hook, observed.** Searches in this category are dominated by third-party comparison lists ("Top 10 Best Buyers Agents in Melbourne", "Compare Top Buyers Advocates"). This is the source type LLMs lean on hardest when asked to recommend a provider. Presence or absence on those lists is checkable, and it splits the field.

On the aggregator lists:

1. **Cate Bakos Property.** Cate Bakos. Books, podcasts, weekly blog, video series, near-total category authority. Target for misdescription risk, not absence: the most-cited person in a category has the most to lose from a wrong or stale AI answer.
2. **Metropole Melbourne.** Michael Yardney. Very large content operation, national profile. Same play as above, and he is a publisher, so he will grasp the mechanism in one sentence.
3. **Buyers Advocate.** Leigh McConnon, MD. Owns a category-generic domain and a list position. Test whether domain strength converts into AI citation.

Spending on their own visibility, absent from the aggregator list checked:

1. **Concierge Buyers Advocates.** Principal shown as Rayson; confirm full name. Flat-fee positioning, "$1B+ purchased", 5-star review badge, ranks for fee queries. Not on the Top 10 list checked.
2. **Melbourne Buyers Advocates** (buyersadvocates-melbourne.com.au). Confirm principal. Exact-match domain, dedicated fees pages, ranks well for cost queries. Not on the Top 10 list checked.

## Vertical 2: Business brokers and M&A advisory

**Fee per client:** commission on a $2M to $25M sale. On a $3M business at 5 to 10 percent, one deal is $150k to $300k. Highest fee per client of any vertical considered.

**The hook, observed directly.** A search for Melbourne business brokers returned firms in Melbourne, **Florida** (Morgan & Westfield, Gulfstream Mergers, "Melbourne FL 32901"). Separately, a "Top 5 Best Business Brokers in Melbourne" article lists four US firms and one actual Melbourne firm. So the source pool an AI would draw on for this category is contaminated by the wrong hemisphere. That is demonstrable in a single screen recording and will surprise every principal on this list.

**Second hook.** "Should I use a broker or sell privately", "what is my business worth", "best business broker Melbourne" are asked privately of AI precisely because owners will not ask them out loud. Same buyer psychology as the legal category, without the personal history.

1. **Mercury Business Sales and Valuation.** Camil Talj. Listings $2.2M to $5.2M, AIBB and CPBB certified, registered valuer, active blog, bilingual content. Spends on authority, so the contamination finding will land.
2. **Vision Brokers and Advisors.** Dan Levitus (confirm). $500k to $25M deals, four offices, knowledge hub, AIBB. National footprint makes the Melbourne-versus-Florida problem directly commercial.
3. **Trident Business Sales.** The one genuinely Melbourne firm on the US-authored top-5 list. Show them the other four are American.
4. **Lloyds Business Brokers.** Corporate end of the market, national brand. Confirm principal.
5. **Expert Business Brokers.** Daniel Callegari and Sam Vasli, both MDs. Deals under $1M plus a separate advisory arm above $1M. Caveat: no blog, no visible reviews, no awards. Thin marketing means the pain may not register yet. Rank last, use as a control.

## Room test

Both verticals are founder-led and commercially literate. Broking has the strongest room test for Lee specifically: he has built and exited businesses, so the conversation starts at peer level rather than vendor level.

## What is verified and what is not

Verified: principals named above, fee ranges, marketing signals, aggregator list membership, and the Florida contamination.

Not verified: how any of these ten actually appear inside a ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or AI Overview answer. That requires running Videt, which is step 2 of the playbook and cannot be done from a web search. No gap gets claimed in outreach until it has been measured.
