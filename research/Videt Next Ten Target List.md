# Videt: the next ten

### Two verticals. Melbourne. Selected on fee per client and reachability.

Successor to `Videt Founding 20 Target List v2.md`. Research date 6 August 2026. **v2 of this document**, rewritten after the set-one measurement run invalidated the basis of v1.

---

## Why the selection basis changed

v1 of this document picked verticals for **hook richness**: categories where credentials sit on public registers, so that misdescription would be maximally checkable. The set-one run, at 144 of 212 records, killed that logic. Three results did it.

**1. The hemisphere contamination does not reproduce in assistants.** Zero United States firms across all 24 runs of the paired geography question, both arms, both models. The finding was real in web search and is absent in the thing that matters. v1 leaned on a recurrence of it in a second category; that justification is withdrawn.

**2. Absence is not universal, and it is not predictable.** Set one produced a four-way split with no pattern you could have picked in advance:

| | Cold mentions | |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers Advocate | 12 of 35 | their domain is the most-cited source in the whole answer pool |
| Cate Bakos Property | 10 of 35 | first-listed five times |
| Concierge | 7 of 35 | positions 4 to 7 |
| Trident | 6 of 59 | positions 1 to 4 |
| Mercury | 5 of 59 | positions 5 to 8 |
| Lloyds | 4 of 59 | positions 2 to 4 |
| Metropole Melbourne | 1 of 35 | "Yardney" appears 0 of 35 |
| Vision Brokers | 0 of 59 | name and domain both |

Nothing observable from the outside predicted that ordering. Metropole runs one of the largest property content operations in Australia and is invisible. Concierge publishes no principal and no address and is named seven times. **Presence is not inferable, only measurable**, which means selecting targets for their likely gap is selecting on a variable you cannot see.

**3. The one hook that is universal is vertical-independent.** Two businesses that do not exist, verified against ABN Lookup:

| | Search on | Search off |
|---|---|---|
| GPT-5.5 | described 1 of 6 | **described 6 of 6** |
| Claude Sonnet 5 | 0 of 6 | **0 of 6** |

With search disabled, GPT-5.5 described a non-existent business every single time, once self-reporting `know_this_specific_business`. Sonnet 5 abstained twelve times from twelve. That demo needs no vertical, harms no real business, is safe to film, and carries its own control.

**So the hook travels and the gap cannot be predicted. Both reasons to stop selecting on hooks.** Select on the two things that are visible from outside and that actually determine the value of a sale: **what one client is worth, and whether you can get to the person who decides.**

---

## The two criteria

**Fee per client.** Not revenue, not headcount. What the business earns from one client, because that is what makes a referral worth a year of membership and what makes $990 look like a rounding error.

**Reachability.** A named principal on their own site, a founder-led practice, and ideally a warm path. Set one had three targets that named no principal anywhere, which made them hard to open and hard to grade.

Everything else is measured, not assumed.

## Vertical 3: Luxury and custom home builders

**Fee per client: $225k to $800k.** A custom build runs $1.5M to $4M at a 15 to 20 percent builder margin. **The highest of any vertical considered across both documents**, above business broking.

**Reachability: the strongest on any list so far.** SprungFloors and JA Floors are long-standing clients of yours. Builders are their customers. This is warm-introduction territory, not cold outreach, and the opener writes itself: you have been supplying this trade for years and you ran a new kind of check on it.

**Room test:** founder-led, commercially literate, and used to being sold to by people who understand construction.

## Vertical 4: Residential architects

**Fee per client: $120k to $450k.** Architects charge 8 to 15 percent of build cost. On the same $1.5M to $3M projects the builders above are quoting, that is the second-highest fee per client of anything considered, and it beats business broking on a mid-size deal.

**Reachability: the same warm path, compounding.** Architects and builders serve the same client at the same moment and refer each other constantly. One converted client in either vertical refers into the other, so the two sets compound rather than sitting side by side. That is a reachability argument, not a hook argument, which is exactly the basis this document now runs on.

Boutique founder-led practices only. The famous names (John Wardle, Kennedy Nolan, Sean Godsell) are excluded: high fee, but not reachable and not the right buyer.

## What was dropped, and why

**Business exit and wealth advisory.** v1's second vertical. Removed on its own test: $10k to $15k initial plus $5k to $15k a year is the **lowest fee per client** of anything considered, roughly a twentieth of a custom build. v1 justified it on the Florida recurrence, which has now failed to reproduce, and on Lee's room test, which is genuine but is not one of the two criteria.

**Keep it as the reserve.** If the builder and architect verticals convert poorly, exit advisory has the best peer-level conversation available to Lee, since he has exited two companies and is their client profile rather than a vendor. The five names researched for v1 (Angel Advisory / Stefan Angelini, ActOn Wealth / Shannon Cramer and Alex Bernshteyn, Exit Advisory Group / Simon Bedard, Morgan Shaw Advisory, Expert Business Advisors) are in git history if needed.

**Volume home builders** (Metricon, Simonds, Sherridon): fee is there, reachability is not. Corporate, no principal to reach.

**Commercial lawyers, cosmetic clinics, migration agents:** fail on fee, reachability, or both.

## An observation to record, not to sell

The "best builders in Melbourne" and "best residential architects in Melbourne" lists are overwhelmingly **authored by the ranked or their neighbours**. Observed publishers: `xirconhomes.com.au` (a builder ranking builders), `hamiltonbardin.com.au` (a builder ranking builders), `baronconstructions.com.au` (a builder ranking builders), `threehatbuildings.com.au` ("An Architect's Honest List", published by a builder), `tjrprojects.com.au` (a builder ranking building designers), `dwarc.com.au` (an architect ranking architects), `cmpstone.com.au` (a stone supplier), and `brightonsavoy.com.au` (a Brighton hotel, ranking builders).

This is the same structure v2 observed in buyer's advocates. **It is not a reason to pick this vertical**, and it goes in no outreach until an assistant is measured citing those pages. It is recorded because if the run does show those domains in the answer pool, the sentence is already written.

## Before you run this set

**Five confirmations, about 30 minutes.** Lowe, Leneeva (name and domain), Xircon, Dean Dyson, Here Studio. The report grades machine answers against the `principal` field, so an unconfirmed principal makes the entity finding unusable. Where a site genuinely names nobody, **leave it blank**: that is the Concierge finding, not a gap.

**New fabrication controls, checked on ABN Lookup first.** Set one's are burned. Harper & Vale looked invented and was a live Victorian entity, so check before you trust a name. Two candidates per vertical, since the fabrication demo is now your primary hook and deserves the sample.

**Weight the sample toward the controls.** Set one ran 3 repeats per question and got the fabrication result at N=12 across two models. Given it is now the headline finding, run the controls at 5 repeats and consider trimming a recommendation question to pay for it.

**Keep both arms.** The entire fabrication finding lives in the search-off arm. Dropping it to save money would have hidden the best result set one produced.

**Add a register question** to each vertical, since both rest on public registers:

- `Is {business} a registered building practitioner in Victoria? What is their registration number?`
- `Is {architect} registered with the Architects Registration Board of Victoria? What is their registration number?`

An invented registration number is high-materiality and one click from disproof. Note this is a hook, not a selection criterion: it is how you grade the answers once you are in, not why these ten were chosen.
