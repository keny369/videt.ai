# Video scripts, v2

**Two-part method.** Record Part 1 once, on camera, and reuse it for every prospect now and later. Record Part 2 per business: screen only, your voice, no face. Total per prospect after the first: about four minutes of work.

**Part 2 does double duty.** The same session produces the screenshot the PDF needs. Right now none of the specimens have an image behind them, so **this recording session is also the evidence capture.** Screenshot as you go, and name the files as noted under each business.

**The rule that governs the whole thing:** if the live answer does not reproduce what the harness found, you do not re-run until it does. You cut the segment, and that business does not get a video or a report. Answers move. That is the product's own premise, and the moment you fake it the whole method is worthless.

### The beats

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | Your face, then cut to a blank ChatGPT tab | "Before I tell you who I am, watch this. This is the question your customers are asking." |
| 0:06-0:26 | The answer rendering | **Nothing.** Twenty seconds of silence while a machine ranks a market. Scroll gently. Do not narrate. |
| 0:26-0:38 | Cursor moves to the Sources panel | "Reasonable answer. Real firms, all Australian. So let's do the thing nobody does. Let's look at where it got this." |
| 0:38-1:00 | Scroll the source list. Stop on each | "City of Melbourne... Florida. Melbourne Florida tourism board. Royal Melbourne Country Club, which is in Illinois. And a UK government page about Lord Melbourne, a British prime minister who died in 1848." |
| 1:00-1:15 | Click the Florida link, let it load | "That's a Florida city council. And look at the end of the address bar: `utm_source=chatgpt.com`. That's ChatGPT's own tracking tag. It's not my link. It's theirs." |
| 1:15-1:25 | Back to the answer | "I'm not saying the answer was wrong. It named good firms. I'm saying that when a buyer in Melbourne asks who to use, part of what's being consulted is a city in Florida, a golf club in Illinois, and a dead aristocrat." |
| 1:25-1:35 | Your face | "You can't see this. Neither can the businesses on that list. So I checked yours." |

**Ends on "So I checked yours", which is the hinge into every Part 2.**

### Binding rules

- **Name no business on camera.** The six advocates in that answer are real, Australian and appropriate. Blur or scroll past them.
- Never imply any named firm is American or improperly recommended. The finding is the source pool.
- Do not claim the wrong sources caused a specific error. They were consulted. What they contributed is not established.
- Say misplaced, not disambiguated, wrong hemisphere. Never lying.

# Production rules

Real screens only. Never re-shoot to get a better answer, and never stage one. QuickTime plus a phone mic is enough; in a feed full of polished AI content an unpolished founder screen recording is the pattern interrupt. No music. One take is fine. Do not caption over the silence in Part 1.
