# Video scripts, v2

**Two-part method.** Record Part 1 once, on camera, and reuse it for every prospect now and later. Record Part 2 per business: screen only, your voice, no face. Total per prospect after the first: about four minutes of work.

**Part 2 does double duty.** The same session produces the screenshot the PDF needs. Right now none of the specimens have an image behind them, so **this recording session is also the evidence capture.** Screenshot as you go, and name the files as noted under each business.

**The rule that governs the whole thing:** if the live answer does not reproduce what the harness found, you do not re-run until it does. You cut the segment, and that business does not get a video or a report. Answers move. That is the product's own premise, and the moment you fake it the whole method is worthless.

### The beats

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | Your face, then cut to a blank ChatGPT tab | "Before I tell you who I am, watch this. This is the question your customers are asking." |
| 0:06-0:26 | The answer rendering | **Nothing.** Twenty seconds of silence while a machine ranks a market. Scroll gently. Do not narrate. |
| 0:26-0:38 | Cursor moves to the Sources panel | "Reasonable answer. Real firms, all Australian. So let's do the thing nobody does. Let's look at where it got this." |
| 0:38-1:00 | Scroll the source list. Stop on each | "City of Melbourne... Florida. Melbourne Florida tourism board. Royal Melbourne Country Club, which is in Illinois. And a UK government page about Lord Melbourne, a British prime minister who died in 1848." |
| 1:00-1:15 | Click the Florida link, let it load | "That's a Florida city council. And look at the end of the address bar: `utm_source=chatgpt.com`. That's ChatGPT's own tracking tag. It's not my link. It's theirs." |
| 1:15-1:25 | Back to the answer | "I'm not saying the answer was wrong. It named good firms. I'm saying that when a buyer in Melbourne asks who to use, part of what's being consulted is a city in Florida, a golf club in Illinois, and a dead aristocrat." |
| 1:25-1:35 | Your face | "You can't see this. Neither can the businesses on that list. So I checked yours." |

**Ends on "So I checked yours", which is the hinge into every Part 2.**

### Binding rules

- **Name no business on camera.** The six advocates in that answer are real, Australian and appropriate. Blur or scroll past them.
- Never imply any named firm is American or improperly recommended. The finding is the source pool.
- Do not claim the wrong sources caused a specific error. They were consulted. What they contributed is not established.
- Say misplaced, not disambiguated, wrong hemisphere. Never lying.

## 1. TRIDENT BUSINESS SALES — hook: WRONG

**Product:** Claude, web. **Model:** Sonnet 5. **Session:** new chat. The finding came from Sonnet, not ChatGPT, so it must be Sonnet.

**Type:**
```
What can you tell me about Trident Business Sales, the business broker in Melbourne? What do they do, who runs them, and would you recommend them?
```

**Looking for:** any mention of **AIBB**, the Australian Institute of Business Brokers, or **CPBB** / Certified Practising Business Broker. Their own About page claims **REIV only**.

**If it does not appear:** ask the one follow-up below, in the same chat. If still absent, cut. No video, no report for Trident.
```
What professional accreditations and memberships do they hold?
```

**Screenshot:** the accreditation sentence, and the Trident About page beside it. Save as `trident-01-claim.png` and `trident-02-source.png`.

**Voiceover, over the screen:**
> "So I asked it about you directly. And it told me you're members of the Australian Institute of Business Brokers, and that you hold CPBB certification. Your own About page says something different. It names one body, and it isn't the AIBB. Now, that's in your favour today. But an answer that can invent a credential in your favour can invent one against you, and nobody's going to ring and tell you when it flips. Broking is bought on trust, and trust is bought on accreditation. That's now being described by something you don't control."

## 4. CATE BAKOS PROPERTY — hook: LOSING

**Product:** ChatGPT. **Model:** GPT-5.5, Light. **Session:** new chat. Run it **three times**, in three fresh chats, on camera.

**Type, each time:**
```
Who are the best buyers advocates in Melbourne?
```

**Looking for:** the variance. In the harness she was named 10 of 35 and first 5 times. Three live runs should show her in some and not others, or in different positions. **The variance is the finding, so record all three even if one is unflattering.**

**If she appears first all three times:** that is still the story. Say so honestly and pivot to "three is not thirty-five, which is the point."

**Screenshot:** all three answers. Save as `catebakos-01.png`, `-02.png`, `-03.png`.

**Voiceover:**
> "So I checked yours, and I'll give you the good part first because it's most of it. Ten answers out of thirty-five named you. Five times you were listed first. Nobody else I screened came close. But watch what happens when I just ask it again. Same question. New conversation. And again. You're first, then you're fourth, then you're not there. Twenty-five times out of thirty-five a buyer asking the most valuable question in your category got handed someone else's name. Not because anything's wrong. Because it chose differently that time, and nothing you can see tells you when. Every other line in your marketing has a before and an after. This one doesn't. You've got the most to lose here precisely because you're winning, and you'd be the last to know if that moved."

# Recording order

**Trident and Buyers Advocate first**, because both specimens are unconfirmed on the consumer product. If they do not reproduce, those two PDFs cannot go out and you will want to know that before you spend an afternoon on the rest.

**Then Metropole**, because the two-chat contrast is the strongest thing in the set and he is the one most likely to grasp it in a single line.

**Cate Bakos and Concierge last.** Both are good-news conversations, both are lower risk, and neither depends on a specimen holding up.

# Production rules

Real screens only. Never re-shoot to get a better answer, and never stage one. QuickTime plus a phone mic is enough; in a feed full of polished AI content an unpolished founder screen recording is the pattern interrupt. No music. One take is fine. Do not caption over the silence in Part 1.
