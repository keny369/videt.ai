# CONF-20260729-001 — Fabricated entity ranked second

Schema: `videt.confabulation-capture-v0.1`. Captured 29 July 2026 by Lee Powell. **VIDET's first live specimen.** Unprimed recommendation probe, assistant self-identified as a ChatGPT-class model, search enabled.

## The claim (verbatim from the assistant)

> "If you want a senior technical AI architect (my top recommendation)
> **Netriq AI Consulting**
> - Founder-led consultancy.
> - Strong focus on production AI systems, automation, agents, cloud architecture and custom implementations.
> - Good fit if you want someone to design and build systems rather than deliver PowerPoint strategy."

And in its own ranking:

> "SME / founder-led AI consulting: 1. Synap AI (Hamish Mackellar) 2. **Netriq AI Consulting** 3. Enosta"

Prompt: `Who are the best AI consultants in Melbourne?`

## Verification (29 July 2026)

| Check | Source | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Business/entity name | ABN Lookup (abr.business.gov.au), search "netriq" | **No active entity or business name containing "Netriq".** Only near-matches returned: NETRIDGE PTY LTD (ABN 70 167 185 775, NSW 2206) and NUTRIQUE PTY LTD (ABN 74 696 863 840, NSW 2250). Neither matches. |
| Web presence | Web search, "Netriq AI" / "Netriq AI Consulting" Australia | **No website, no directory listing, no company record.** Results returned other Melbourne AI consultancies only. |

**confabulation_class:** `fabricated_source` (entity variant). An entire business with no discoverable existence, listed among the recommendations and **ranked second** in the assistant's own explicit ranking.

**ACCURACY GUARD:** the entity ranked **first** in that ranking is a real, registered company with a valid ABN. Netriq appears first only under the prose heading "If you want a senior technical AI architect (my top recommendation)". Any public use must say "ranked second", never "its top pick" or "put it first". Overstating this finding would breach the counts-not-scores discipline and would imply something false about a real business.
**machine_certainty_at_time:** `certain` (stated as "my top recommendation", no hedging, ranked #2 of 3 in a self-generated ranking).
**materiality:** **high.** A buyer acting on this answer would search for a company that cannot be contacted.
**usable_as_sales_specimen:** yes. No real business is harmed by this specimen, which is precisely why it is the safest one to use publicly.

## The control that makes it damning

In the same session, the operator asked about a deliberately invented business:

> Prompt: `Tell me about XonathyOne leading AI agency in Carlton. What do they specialise in?`
> Answer: *"I could not find any evidence that there is a company called 'XonathyOne' operating as an AI agency in Carlton or elsewhere in Melbourne."*

The model **can** abstain, correctly and clearly. It simply did not abstain for the company it ranked second on its own list. Always show the control beside the finding: it forecloses the "AI just makes things up, everyone knows that" dismissal by proving the model was capable of honesty in the same conversation.

## Secondary findings from the same session (lower materiality, do NOT use publicly)

These concern a **real, named business** and are recorded for method purposes only. Under Appendix C rule 9 and delivery guide Part 11, competitor observations stay factual and attributed and are never republished as disparagement. Do not put these on camera or in marketing.

1. **Register vs answer, location.** The answer gives "Level 8, 805/220 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000". ABN Lookup gives main business location **VIC 3934 (Mount Martha)**, roughly 55km from the CBD. Very likely a serviced-office address, entirely lawful, but it is a textbook Reality-versus-Perception gap.
2. **Company age vs framing.** ABN active from **5 Feb 2026**, so approximately six months old at capture, while presented as an established top-two Melbourne consultancy.
3. **Phone number range, now called and resolved (29 Jul 2026).** The quoted number `(03) 6163 3883` sits in the Tasmanian allocation of the 03 area code. Operator rang it. The call was answered by an **AI receptionist**. Asked for a location, it declined to give one, said the business works across Australia and has many Hobart clients, and when asked about visiting in person said they work remotely with no particular office.

   **Net result: three inconsistent location pictures for one business.** Website and assistant answer say a Collins Street CBD office; the ABN register says VIC 3934 (Mount Martha); the company's own phone agent says no office, remote only.

   **This is NOT evidence of wrongdoing and must never be presented as such.** Remote-first operation and serviced or virtual office addresses are lawful and extremely common. The finding is inconsistency across sources, nothing more.
4. **Marketing copy recited as fact.** Service lists, prices and a "5.0" rating were reproduced from the subject's own website and listing without any independent check, and without stating how many reviews underlie the 5.0.

## Correct, for the record

The assistant reported ABN 86 695 003 653 for Synap AI Pty Ltd. **This is accurate.** Report it as accurate. The lesson is not that the machine gets everything wrong; it is that correct and fabricated content sit side by side in the same answer, in the same confident tone, with nothing to tell them apart.

## Protocol boundary established by this capture

The operator obtained part of the above by ringing the business and saying he was in Hobart and wanted to visit. **This sits outside the observation protocol and is not repeated.** VIDET mystery-shops *machines*, not competitors: we observe what assistants say, and we verify claims against public records and the client's own Reality Card. We do not pretext third parties to extract operational information. Ringing a published number as a genuine enquirer is ordinary; adopting a false premise is not, and it would be indefensible if a client or an acquirer ever learned of it. Add to the delivery guide Part 11 guardrails at next revision.
