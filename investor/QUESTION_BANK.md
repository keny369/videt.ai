# VIDET — Buyer-Intent Question Bank

Status: Protocol v0 companion (see `30_DAY_REVENUE_PLAN.md`, Appendix D), prepared 27 July 2026. Australian phrasing; `{suburb}` / `{city}` / `{business}` are fill-ins per client. This bank is a menu, not a script — select per the recipe below, then personalise with the client's actual service lines.

## Selection recipe (per assessment, ~22 questions)

| Class | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Recommendation | 8 | Across 3 phrasings × 3 location granularities (suburb / city / "near me") |
| B. Problem-first | 5 | The client's highest-value situations — no category word in the question |
| E. Validation | 3 | The client's own name, plus one common misspelling |
| C. Attribute/niche | 2 | Their genuine differentiators only |
| F/G. Criteria or price | 2 | The machine's teaching answers |
| Controls | 2 | A service they don't offer + a neighbouring suburb they don't serve — calibrates the assistant's willingness to name anyone |

Sampling per Protocol v0: each question ≥3 runs per assistant, fresh sessions, spread over ≥2 days. One follow-up chain per session (e.g. "which of those would you pick for {persona}?") — follow-ups are where shortlists collapse to one name.

## The nine archetypes (universal template)

- **A. Recommendation:** who's the best {type} in {place} · recommend a {type} near {suburb} · top 3 {type} in {city} and why · I'm looking for a {type} in {area}, who should I consider
- **B. Problem-first:** {symptom/situation described}, who can help / what should I do
- **C. Attribute/niche:** {type} in {place} that {fixed fees / speaks {language} / female practitioner / works Saturdays / specialises in {niche} / NDIS-registered}
- **D. Comparison:** compare {A} vs {B} · give me 3 options with pros and cons
- **E. Validation:** is {business} any good · {business} reviews · is {business} licensed/registered/accredited
- **F. Criteria:** how do I choose a {type} · what should I look for · questions to ask before hiring
- **G. Price:** how much does {service} cost in {city} · typical fees for {service} · is {quote} reasonable
- **H. Urgency/logistics:** {type} open Saturday near {suburb} · emergency {service} {suburb} now · who can start this week
- **I. Hire-or-not:** do I need a {type} for {situation} or can I do it myself

## Controls (2 per assessment — non-negotiable)

1. **Wrong-service control:** ask for a service the client does not offer ("Who's the best {unrelated service} in {their suburb}?"). Calibrates how freely the assistant names businesses when it shouldn't have a strong answer.
2. **Wrong-area control:** ask the client's exact service in a suburb they don't serve. If they appear there too, the assistant is pattern-matching, not knowing — which reframes what presence in their own suburb means.

## Follow-up chains (one per session, after any Class A answer)

- "Which of those would you pick for {persona: a family with young kids / a tradie / a first-timer}?"
- "Why those three — what are they known for?"
- "Any I should avoid?" (capture with care — report factually, never republish disparagement)
- "Give me their contact details." (tests whether the machine gets *facts* right — feeds the wrong-facts table)
