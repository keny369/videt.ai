## Lane decision

**Post in: Partnership.** Buyers of assessment slots are fellow owners browsing growth deals. NOT StartUp Pitch (attracts co-founder/equity seekers, not customers). NOT Capital Raising (invites investor diligence — conflicts with "sizzle, not machinery"). **Referrals lane = a second, later listing** to recruit referral partners (accountants/coaches/bookkeepers at a set %) — needs the referral % decided and Appendix G.4 terms first; do not mix audiences in one ad.

## Claim-discipline fixes applied in synthesis (do not regress)

1. Draft line "Whatever it says, that's what every buyer who asks sees" — **cut** (answers vary run to run; that's the product's own premise). Replaced with "ask again tomorrow; the answers move."
2. Draft line "If your name came up, close this ad. You're fine." — **cut the verdict** (one query can't establish "fine"). Kept the dare.
3. Draft line "it becomes A$1,490 after the first twenty" — **cut** (future price stated as fact). Replaced with "Founding price A$990 (standard A$1,490)."
4. Fabricated-event opener ("Somewhere this week, a buyer asked…It answered with a name") — **not used**; the hero line runs in its checkable form: "…asked an AI first. I know, because I checked."
