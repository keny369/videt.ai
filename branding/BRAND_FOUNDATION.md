## 1. Name — VIDET

**Videt** (pronounced *VEE-det* / `ˈviːdɛt`) `[owner-confirm pronunciation]`.

- **Company / brand:** Videt.
- **Domain:** videt.ai.
- **Wordmark:** `Videt` (see `002 VISUAL_IDENTITY.md` for type treatment; the visual system — "Meridian, the Observatory" — is name-agnostic and adopts Videt without redesign).
- **Internal engineering codename remains `F1`** and the ratified product label remains **Discoverability Intelligence Platform** (ADR-003, PR-REQ-001). Videt is the *market* name; it never appears in the specification or the codebase (§11).

## 2. Origin `[owner-confirm]`

*Videt* is the third-person singular present of the Latin *vidēre*, "to see": **"he, she, or it sees."**

The origin is the product argument compressed to one word. The platform exists because a machine now *sees* every business and decides, from what it sees, whether to recommend it. The old question was "how do I rank?"; the new one is "what does the machine see when it looks at me — and will it choose me?" *Videt* names the actor in that sentence: **it sees.** And Videt the company is the instrument that lets a business see what the machine sees, measured against what is true.

The root is a quiet asset: *vidēre* is the ancestor of *vision*, *evident*, *visible*, *video* ("I see"), *provide* and *providence* (*pro-vidēre*, "to see ahead"). The brand can draw on "evident," "in plain sight," and "foresight" without ever explaining the Latin.

> If the owner has a specific origin narrative (a different etymology, an acronym, or a founding story), it supersedes this section, which is otherwise the canonical account.

## 3. Meaning

Three readings, all true at once, in priority order:

1. **It sees.** The machine sees your business. That perception — not your website, not your intentions — is now the commercial reality.
2. **See it.** Videt makes the invisible visible: it shows you what the machines believe, measured against what's true, with evidence you can inspect.
3. **Foresight.** *Pro-vidēre.* Videt is prospective — you observe in order to act, intervene, re-observe, and compound what you learn.

The through-line from `000`/`001`: *your business exists twice — as it is, and as the machines believe it is. The gap between them decides whether your name comes up.* Videt is the Observatory for that gap.

## 4. Mission

**Make commercial machine perception measurable, defensible, and improvable — so any business can see how intelligent systems see it, and change the answer.**

Supporting objectives (from `000`/`001`, unchanged):
- Replace intuition with evidence; opinion with observation; isolated optimisation with compounding learning.
- Every score traces to inspectable evidence; missing data is never synthesised.
- Reports depreciate; knowledge compounds. The enduring asset is the accumulating, evidence-backed intelligence.

## 5. Category

- **Ratified product label (specification-canonical):** Discoverability Intelligence Platform (ADR-003, PR-REQ-001). Used in the spec and in precise/technical/enterprise contexts.
- **Category narrative (the story the site claims and owns):** the **Commercial Discoverability Observatory** (VISION-001). Descriptive; used once per page, subordinate to the customer's problem — never the headline.
- **Emerging framing (owner, watching S-03/S-04 land):** *the operating system for commercial machine perception.* A larger, defensible framing worth developing `[owner-confirm]`: "commercial discoverability intelligence" is the **capability**; **Videt** is the **company**; the **Videt Platform** is the **product**.

The four eras remain the category spine: *first they listed you (directories), then they ranked you (search), then they learned about you (knowledge graphs), now they answer for you (generative intelligence).*

## 6. Positioning

- **Promise (roof):** **Become the answer.** (PR-REQ-003 — spec-canonical, ownable.)
- **One-sentence definition:** *Videt shows you what the machines believe about your business — and measures it against what's true, with evidence behind every number.*
- **Message house (three pillars, from `001`, unchanged):**
  1. **Measurement you can defend** — deterministic, reproducible, seven scored pillars, every Issue traces to evidence, nothing synthesised.
  2. **Answers, not dashboards** — decision-ready guidance ranked by impact, confidence and effort; every recommendation a complete work order.
  3. **Trust by construction** — Videt never touches your systems; ownership is verified before anything is measured; crawling is polite and bounded; access is deny-by-default.
- **Tone of the position:** a structural shift stated calmly (Stripe-on-infrastructure), never fear-marketed. The reader supplies the urgency.

## 7. Voice

From `001`/`002`, unchanged and now the canonical voice standard:
- **Calm and exact.** Authority is implied, never performed.
- **Evidence-calm.** Claims are quiet; presence is full. Restraint governs clutter and claims, never scale.
- **Plain over clever.** Say the category's name in the flagship artifact; keep it subordinate everywhere else.
- **Never fear-marketing.** No "you're invisible and dying." State the shift; let the reader react.
- **Claim discipline.** Every product claim carries its governing identifier (PR-REQ / PRULE / ADR); vision-layer capabilities carry non-commitment labelling (SB-REQ-029).

## 8. Nomenclature (the naming system)

The canonical resolution of "Commercial Discoverability X" placeholders to Videt marks. **Retain the descriptive term in body copy**; lead with the Videt mark.

| Placeholder / descriptive | Videt mark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial Discoverability Platform | **The Videt Platform** | The product. Spec label stays "Discoverability Intelligence Platform." |
| Commercial Discoverability Observatory | **Videt Observatory** | The category narrative device. |
| Commercial Discoverability Intelligence | **Videt Intelligence™** | The compounding IP asset (FUTURE-IP-001). Descriptive "discoverability intelligence" stays lowercase in body. |
| The company | **Videt** | Never "the Videt platform" when the *company* is meant. |
| The score / assessment | **Discoverability Score**, **the assessment** | Spec-canonical; not rebranded. |

Rule: **coined Videt marks are capitalised and may take ™; the descriptive category language ("commercial discoverability", "discoverability intelligence", "machine perception") is lowercase and generic** and must stay usable by the market — a category you want to own is one you let others describe.

## 9. Terminology (canonical vocabulary — do not synonymise)

Brand-facing terms that carry fixed meaning (mirroring the spec's ubiquitous-language discipline):

- **Reality** — what is objectively true about a business.
- **Perception** — what a given AI provider believes about it (provider-specific).
- **Intelligence** — validated, evidence-backed learning accumulated over time (compounds).
- **Observatory** — the instrument that watches perception and measures the gap.
- **Evidence** — the inspectable basis behind every number (spec: immutable, restricted where required).
- **Benchmark** — a permanent, inspectable mark: *this value was measured, here, and you can check it.*
- **The gap** — the distance between Reality and Perception; the thing Videt measures.

One term, one meaning. Marketing may not coin a synonym for a term the specification already fixes (e.g., use **Source**, **Project**, **Organization**, **Recommendation** exactly as the domain model defines them when referring to product mechanics).

## 10. Product naming conventions

- **Product surfaces / features** take plain, descriptive, spec-aligned names (Discoverability Score, Recommendations, Reassessment, Export) — not sub-brands. Avoid a zoo of trademarked feature names.
- **The one elevated asset** is **Videt Intelligence™** (the graph/IP), because it is the moat and deserves a proper-noun.
- **No versioned or animal/space code-names in market copy.** Internal engineering versions (`v1.x`, slice ids `S-0N`, `WF-0NN`, `CAP-0NN`, `PRULE-0NN`, the `F1` codename) are engineering vocabulary and never surface in brand copy.
- **Casing:** `Videt` (title case), `videt.ai` (lowercase). Never `VIDET` in running text except the wordmark.

## 11. Architecture naming conventions (the firewall)

**Binding, per owner direction (2026-07-24): Videt is branding only. It never enters the engineering artifact.**

- **No brand name in namespaces, class names, module names, file names, database objects, environment names, or configuration keys.** The code stays `F1` / `Workflows::WfNNN` / `IdentityAccess::…` / canonical domain terms.
- **No brand name in workflow identifiers (`WF-0NN`), capability identifiers (`CAP-0NN`), product-rule identifiers (`PRULE-0NN`), event names, aggregate names, reason codes, or acceptance-criteria ids.** These are ratified Genesis vocabulary.
- **No brand name in the specification** (`specification/**`), the engineering manual (`engineering/**`), or migrations — unless a ratified Volume I change explicitly introduces it, which is a controlled ADR, not an implementation or branding decision.
- **Canonical domain terms are brand-neutral by design** (Organization, Account, Project, Source, Document, Crawl, Evaluation, Issue, Evidence, Recommendation). The brand describes them for the market; it does not rename them in the model.
- **`branding/` stays a parallel track** — untracked in the engineering history, never staged into engineering commits, never referenced by `app/**`, `db/**`, `specification/**`.

This firewall is what lets the brand evolve (VOI → Videt, and whatever comes next) without a single code or contract change.

## 12. Trademark guidance `[owner-confirm — not legal advice]`

- **Assert ™ on coined marks:** Videt™, Videt Intelligence™. (™ is common-law; ® only after registration.)
- **Do not ™ the descriptive category** ("commercial discoverability", "discoverability intelligence", "become the answer" is a tagline — assess separately) — trademarking the descriptor undermines category ownership and is hard to defend.
- **Consider registration** for `Videt` (word mark) and the logo, in the classes covering SaaS / data analysis / marketing-analytics software, in the primary markets (spec markets: US, UK, AU, NZ, CA, ZA per OD-011).
- **Clear the name** before spend: existing "Videt" marks/entities, the `videt.*` domain landscape, and app-store/social handle availability.
- **Route all of this through qualified counsel.** This section is a starting brief, not legal advice.

## 13. Domain strategy `[owner-confirm]`

- **Primary:** `videt.ai` (secured). The `.ai` TLD reads as intentional for this category.
- **Defensive registrations (recommended):** `videt.com` (acquire if feasible — the credibility/primary-market default), `videt.io`, `getvidet.com`, plus the primary-market ccTLDs (`videt.co.uk`, `videt.com.au`, …) and the obvious typo/adjacent forms.
- **Redirects:** all defensive domains 301 to `videt.ai`; email on the primary; no split-brand hosting.
- **Handles:** secure `@videt` / `videt` / `videtai` across the platforms that matter for B2B (LinkedIn, X, GitHub org, npm/product-hunt if relevant) before launch.
- **The VOI → Videt cutover** (voi.ai) is an owner decision: hold, redirect, or retire the prior domain and handles. Not started here.
